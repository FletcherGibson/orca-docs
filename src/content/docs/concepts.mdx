---
title: Concepts
---

## Run
A durable record of a blueprint or work-pipeline execution.

Runs persist state, terminal decisions, and evidence artifacts.

## Blueprint
A declarative workflow definition that Orca can validate and run.

Blueprints define:
- the workflow steps
- retry behavior
- terminal outcomes
- evidence-producing execution paths

## Decision
Every blueprint run ends as one of:
- `PASS`
- `WARN`
- `BLOCK`

## Role / worker
The configured execution identity used for automated work.

Workers operate in isolated worktrees and write logs and evidence artifacts.

## Evidence
Structured output that explains what happened during a run and why it passed, warned, or blocked.
