---
title: Concepts
---

## Run
A durable record of a blueprint execution.

Runs persist state, terminal decisions, and evidence artifacts.

## Blueprint
A declarative workflow definition that Orca can validate and run.

Blueprints define:
- the workflow steps
- retry behavior
- terminal outcomes
- evidence-producing execution paths
- how deterministic and non-deterministic work are combined

## Decision
Every blueprint run ends as one of:
- `PASS`
- `WARN`
- `BLOCK`

## Deterministic step
A step whose execution contract is explicit and machine-checkable.

Examples:
- `cmd`
- `ci`
- `check`
- `wait`

## Non-deterministic step
A step that involves judgment, authorship, or approval.

Examples:
- `agent`
- `approve`

These steps can still be auditable because Orca persists the rendered prompt, command output, and final decision in the run evidence.

## Evidence
Structured output that explains what happened during a run and why it passed, warned, or blocked.
